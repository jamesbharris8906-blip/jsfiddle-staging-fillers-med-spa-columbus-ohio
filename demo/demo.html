Frequently Asked Questions

How much do fillers cost? Cost depends on the area, the product, and how much is used. Your provider will give you a clear price at your consultation, before you decide. Ask what is included, and whether you will need a touch-up visit.

Will I still look like me? Yes, if the work is done well. A good provider uses small amounts, checks your face as they go, and aims for a soft, natural result. Bring a photo of yourself from a few years ago if it helps. Tell them what you want to keep, not just what you want to change.

How long do fillers last? Lips often last six to twelve months, and cheeks, chin, and jawline can last a year or more. Botox lasts three to four months. Your provider will give you a clear range for your treatment.

Can I get fillers and Botox on the same day? Yes, many people do. Botox treats lines from movement, and fillers add volume. Your provider can do both in one visit, or space them out, based on what fits your face.

What if I do not like the result? Most hyaluronic acid fillers can be dissolved with an enzyme. That is one reason they are a good first choice. Talk with your provider about it before you book, so you know your choices.

Do I need to stop any medicines before fillers? Tell your provider about all medicines, vitamins, and supplements you take. Some, like blood thinners, can add to bruising. Your provider will tell you what to do before your visit.
